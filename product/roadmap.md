# Product Roadmap

## v1 – Explainable Risk Scoring
- Agent-level composite risk score
- Top behavioral drivers with impact levels
- AI-generated narrative summary
- Manual review workflow

## v2 – Performance Monitoring & Feedback
- Trend tracking over time
- Coaching feedback loops
- Threshold alerts for behavioral changes
- Before/after comparisons following interventions

## v3 – Workflow Integration
- Embedded views within analytics dashboards
- Automated reporting and exports
- Cross-team visibility for underwriting and pricing
- Audit-ready explanation history

The roadmap emphasizes incremental value delivery while maintaining transparency and trust.
