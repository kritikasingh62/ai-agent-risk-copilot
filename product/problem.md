# Problem Statement

Agent risk evaluation within the Auto Line of Business has historically relied on loss ratio–based analytics and predictive modeling.

While statistically valid in aggregate, this approach introduced several challenges at the agent level:

- Loss ratios are highly volatile at low volumes
- Small claim events disproportionately skew results
- Model outputs were difficult to interpret and explain
- Stakeholders lacked confidence in why agents were flagged as risky

As a result, teams spent more time debating model outputs than acting on insights.

The core problem was not insufficient modeling sophistication, but a lack of **clarity, explainability, and shared understanding** across underwriting, pricing, and product teams.

The opportunity was to design a system that:
- Focuses on behavioral performance rather than prediction
- Produces interpretable, actionable insights
- Aligns with how decisions are actually made in practice
